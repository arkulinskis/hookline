What makes a hook work

It's short enough to finish before "see more" (about 140 characters on a phone), specific (a number, a moment, a name), and it opens a loop: a tension, a surprise, or a promise the post keeps. Score is a rule-of-thumb, not a prediction.

Hookline only drafts: you read, edit and post the comment yourself. Automated comments break LinkedIn's rules and are shown to fewer people.

LinkedIn has no real bold. The "bold" generators swap letters for maths symbols, which screen readers announce one by one and LinkedIn search can't match. Use a short first line and white space for emphasis instead.
