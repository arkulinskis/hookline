Bring your numbers

LinkedIn lets you download your own analytics. Hookline reads that file.

  1. On LinkedIn, open your profile → Analytics (Post impressions).
  2. Choose the date range (up to 365 days) and press Export.
  3. Drop the .xlsx file here.

You can also log results per post: open a posted post → Results.
