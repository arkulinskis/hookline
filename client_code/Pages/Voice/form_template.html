Your own Claude API key

Hookline is free because you bring the AI. Your key is saved only in this browser and sent straight to Anthropic: Hookline's server never sees it or your drafts. A post costs well under a cent.

  1. Go to the Claude Console (console.anthropic.com) → API keys → Create key.
  2. Add a little credit under Billing (and a monthly limit if you like).
  3. Paste the key here.

What the AI does here

Drafts from your ideas, rewrites in your voice, hooks, comment drafts, repurposing and carousel slides, using your voice card and rules (no invented facts: it leaves [brackets] for you). It never posts anything. Without a key, every page still works with the built-in coach.

Connect through LinkedIn's official sign-in. Hookline gets permission to publish posts only when you press Post now. It can't read your messages, connections or feed, and it never posts on its own.

One-click posting isn't switched on for this Hookline yet. You can still post everything with Copy and open LinkedIn: Hookline copies your post and opens LinkedIn's share box.

Why no auto-posting and no extension?

LinkedIn's API terms don't allow tools to automate posting, and its User Agreement bans browser add-ons that scrape or act for you. Tools that ignored this have had accounts restricted and extensions shut down. Hookline stays on the official, safe side: you schedule, it reminds you, you press the button.
