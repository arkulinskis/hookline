Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, every feature. The AI is optional and runs on your own Anthropic key, so you pay Anthropic directly, usually well under a cent per post. That's how Hookline stays free for everyone.

Will this get my LinkedIn account restricted?

Hookline uses only LinkedIn's official API, and only when you press Post now. No browser extension, no stored LinkedIn password, no scraping, no auto-comments or auto-DMs: the things LinkedIn's User Agreement forbids.

Can it post on a schedule?

It reminds you at the time you pick, and you post with one click. LinkedIn's API terms don't allow tools to automate posting, so Hookline doesn't.

Where do the analytics come from?

From you: LinkedIn lets you export your own analytics as a spreadsheet. Drop it into Insights, or log a post's numbers by hand.

Does it write in French?

The AI writes in whatever language you set in your voice. The post checks (reading level, clichés) are tuned for English for now.

Where is my key stored?

Only in your browser. Requests go straight from your browser to Anthropic; Hookline's server never sees the key or your AI prompts.
